Privacy Policy — Fitness Local

Effective date: the date of first publication.

The short version

Fitness Local works entirely on your phone. It does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data — there is no account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it yourself.

Data we collect

None. The app sends no data to us or to any third party. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no way to see, access, or delete your data — it lives only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Everything the app knows about you — your profile, body measurements, meal plans, workouts, weigh-ins, and settings — is stored in an encrypted database on your own phone (SQLCipher encryption). It is readable only on that device.

Backups

The app can create an encrypted backup file. The backup is protected with AES-256-GCM encryption, and the encryption key is derived from a password you choose (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations). We cannot recover your backup password — if you lose it, the backup cannot be restored. Store your backups and your password somewhere safe.

Exports (CSV)

You can export your data as CSV files. CSV exports are plain text and are not encrypted — anyone who can read the file can read the data. Handle exported files accordingly, and delete them when you no longer need them.

Purchases

The app is sold as a one-time purchase through the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. All payment processing is handled by Google or Apple under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive no payment information from them.

Permissions

The app may request device permissions only where needed for features you use (for example, storage access to save or restore backup and CSV files). Permissions are used solely for that purpose.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone — children included — because we collect no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address, and the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
